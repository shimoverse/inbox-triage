Inbox Triage

Shows your Inbox Triage dashboard at the top of Gmail and asks your Inbox Triage server to sort new mail as it arrives. The extension holds no Google or AI keys and never reads, sends or changes mail itself.

Server

Use https, or http://127.0.0.1:8765 if you run Inbox Triage on this computer.

Connected Gmail accounts

    To connect an account, open Gmail and click Connect Gmail in the Inbox Triage bar.
